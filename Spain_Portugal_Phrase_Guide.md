## Coffee

| Phrase | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Un café con leche | oon kah-FEH kohn LEH-cheh | Coffee with milk (closest to a latte) |
| Un cortado | oon kor-TAH-doh | Espresso with a small splash of milk |
| Leche sin lactosa | LEH-cheh seen lahk-TOH-sah | Lactose-free milk |
| ¿Tiene leche sin lactosa? | tee-EH-neh LEH-cheh seen lahk-TOH-sah | Do you have lactose-free milk? |

**Note:** Ordering "un café" alone defaults to a small black espresso. There's no native "flat white" — at a specialty coffee shop you can usually just say "flat white" in English, or order a cortado in a traditional café for something similar in ratio.

## Numbers / Pricing

| # | Spanish |
|---|---|
| 1–10 | uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco, seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez |
| 20 / 30 / 40 / 50 / 60 / 70 / 80 / 90 | veinte, treinta, cuarenta, cincuenta, sesenta, setenta, ochenta, noventa |
| 100 / 500 / 1,000 | cien, quinientos, mil |

**Note:** For 21+, use **y** ("and") between tens and units — 31 = treinta y uno, 45 = cuarenta y cinco. "Cien" becomes "ciento" once followed by more digits: 154 = **ciento cincuenta y cuatro**. 500 is irregular (quinientos, not "cinco cientos"). Prices use a comma for decimals — 12,50 € is read "doce con cincuenta."

## Getting Around

| Phrase | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Izquierda | eeth-kee-EHR-dah | Left |
| Derecha | deh-REH-chah | Right |
| Todo recto | TOH-doh REHK-toh | Straight ahead |
| Aquí / Allí | ah-KEE / ah-YEE | Here / There |
| Cerca / Lejos | SEHR-kah / LEH-hohs | Near / Far |
| ¿Dónde está...? | DOHN-deh es-TAH | Where is...? |
| ¿Cómo llego a...? | KOH-moh YEH-goh ah | How do I get to...? |
| La parada (de autobús) | lah pah-RAH-dah (deh ow-toh-BOOS) | The (bus) stop |
| La estación | lah es-tah-see-OHN | The station |
| Un taxi | oon TAHK-see | A taxi |
| Con permiso | kohn per-MEE-soh | Excuse me (to pass by) |
| No pasa nada | no PAH-sah NAH-dah | No worries / it's fine |
| De nada | deh NAH-dah | You're welcome |

**Note:** "Con permiso" is your best friend in crowded pintxos bars — say it while gently moving through, more natural than "perdón" for that specific situation.

## Money & Shopping

| Phrase | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| La cuenta, por favor | lah KWEN-tah poor fah-VOR | Check, please |
| ¿Tarjeta? | tar-HEH-tah | Card? (simplified — just hold it up) |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta? | KWAHN-toh KWES-tah | How much is this? |
| ¿Tiene uno más grande/pequeño? | tee-EH-neh OO-noh mahs GRAHN-deh/peh-KEH-nyoh | Do you have a bigger/smaller one? |

**Note:** Tipping isn't expected in Spain — rounding up is generous, never required. The pen-signing hand gesture for "check, please" is understood here too.

# 🇵🇹 PORTUGUESE (Portugal)

## Basics

| Phrase | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Por favor / Se faz favor | poor fah-VOR | Please |
| Sim | seeng | Yes |
| Não | nowng (nasal) | No |
| Desculpe | dish-KOOL-peh | Sorry / Excuse me |
| Não entendo / Não percebo | nowng en-TEN-doo | I don't understand |
| Não sei mais português | nowng say mighsh por-too-GESH | I don't know any more Portuguese |
| Só sei um pouco de português | soh say oong POH-koo deh por-too-GESH | I only know a little Portuguese |
| Mais devagar, por favor | mighsh deh-vah-GAHR poor fah-VOR | More slowly, please |
| Fala inglês? | FAH-lah een-GLESH | Do you speak English? |
| Isto | EESH-too | This (pointing) |
| Obrigada / Obrigado | oh-bree-GAH-dah / doo | Thank you (**you** say obrigada, **your partner** says obrigado — based on speaker's own gender, said constantly) |
| Pode ser? / Está bem? | POH-deh sehr / esh-TAH bang | Okay? |
| Um momento, por favor | oong moh-MEN-too poor fah-VOR | One moment, please |

| Adoro isto | ah-DOH-roo EESH-too | I love this |

## Coffee

| Phrase | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Um galão | oong gah-LOWNG | Milky coffee in a glass (closest to a latte) |
| Uma meia de leite | OO-mah MAY-ah deh LAY-teh | Coffee with milk, smaller than a galão |
| Leite sem lactose | LAY-teh sang lahk-TOHZ | Lactose-free milk |
| Tem leite sem lactose? | tang LAY-teh sang lahk-TOHZ | Do you have lactose-free milk? |

**Note:** "Um café" alone defaults to a small espresso (called a *bica* in Lisbon). No native "flat white" — try a specialty coffee shop and ask by name, or order a meia de leite for something in that texture range.

## Numbers / Pricing

| # | Portuguese |
|---|---|
| 1–10 | um, dois, três, quatro, cinco, seis, sete, oito, nove, dez |
| 20 / 30 / 50 / 100 | vinte, trinta, cinquenta, cem |
| 500 / 1,000 | quinhentos, mil |

**Note:** Use **e** ("and") between tens and units — 154 = **cento e cinquenta e quatro**. 500 is irregular (quinhentos). Prices also use a comma for decimals — 12,50 € is read "doze e cinquenta."

## Getting Around

**Note:** People often respond to "obrigada" with "de nada" or just a nod — no need to over-thank.
